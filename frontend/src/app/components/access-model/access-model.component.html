Special — Selected Credentials
A lane alongside the ladder for First Nations, proponents, certificate holders, partner agencies and local governments
Grants a named external party sight of specified content at Levels 1–3 without making them a member of that audience. The file's level does not change and no one else's access changes with it. Time-bound, renewable, revocable — singly or in bulk.
